## 4. Key Systems & Modules

The software is an integrated suite of the following specialized modules:

### 👤 1. Comprehensive Member Management
* **Member Profiles:** Full lifecycle tracking (registration, status, contact details).
* **Automated Enrollments:** Assign members seamlessly to specific Programs and Classes.
* **Bulk Imports:** Effortlessly import legacy member data using Excel/CSV.

### 💰 2. Billing & Financial Tracking
* **Payment Processing:** Record and track all fee payments, membership dues, and custom payment items.
* **Payment Schedules:** Automated tracking of member payment schedules and due dates.
* **Arrears Management:** Identify outstanding balances instantly.

### 📅 3. Program, Class & Schedule Management
* **Complex Hierarchies:** Create primary Programs (e.g., Swimming, Tennis) and nested Classes (e.g., Beginners, Advanced).
* **Cancellations & Holidays:** Systematically manage Class or Practice cancellations and block out Holidays.
* **Locations Management:** Map classes and sessions to multiple locations or physical venues.
* **Special Bookings:** Handle exclusive or customized bookings outside regular schedules.

### 🏫 4. Coach & Staff Management
* **Coach Profiles:** Manage instructors, their contact details, and assigned programs.
* **Workload Tracking:** Analyze how many classes and students each coach is managing.

### 🕒 5. Automated Attendance (Biometric Ready)
* **Real-time Tracking:** Daily attendance logging linked exactly to specific classes and members.
* **Face Recognition Integration:** Ready to sync with advanced biometric Face Recognition devices for a completely touchless, fully automated attendance workflow.

### 📩 6. SMS Notifications & Communication
* **Custom SMS Templates:** Create dynamic SMS templates.
* **Automated Alerts:** Contact members automatically regarding payment reminders, class cancellations, holiday notices, and special announcements.

### 📊 7. Dashboard & Advanced Analytics
* **Insights at a Glance:** Live dashboard showcasing revenue generation, active members, attendance trends, and pending tasks.
* **Activity Logs (Audit Trail):** Full visibility into system usage—know exactly who logged in and what actions they performed for maximum security accountability.
